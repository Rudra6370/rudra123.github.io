Why Routinix?

Your Health & Habit Data Stays 100% Private

✓

Local-First Storage

All your reminders, habits and health data are stored on your device using Hive & SharedPreferences. Nothing is sent to external servers.

✓

No Account Required

Start using Routinix instantly. No sign-up, no cloud sync, no data breaches to worry about.

✓

Battery-Friendly Alarms

Uses Android Alarm Manager for reliable, exact alarms that reschedule after reboot and won't drain your battery.

✓

Backup & Restore

Export your data as a backup file and restore it anytime. You're always in control.

🛡️

Your Data Never Leaves Your Device

We believe your health and habit data is deeply personal. That's why Routinix keeps everything local.
